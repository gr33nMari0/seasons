Open your server's dashboard

Type the address of your Minecraft server's Seasons dashboard. Your server's owner can tell you what it is; it often starts with seasons.

What happens

  1. This page asks your server whether it runs a Seasons dashboard, at https://<address>/.well-known/seasons.json.
  2. If it does, your browser goes straight to that dashboard. Everything after that, logging in included, happens on your server's own site.

This site stores nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere but the address you type. It has no cookies, no trackers and no scripts from other sites.
