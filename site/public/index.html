Seasons
AboutConnect

Every season ends. Its legends stay in the Hall of Fame.

Seasons runs a season format on one Minecraft world that never resets. When a season ends, everyone's gear is archived into a Hall of Fame, everyone starts again at a fresh spawn in new land, and the old world stays locked until the group votes for Homecoming.

Open your server's dashboard

Hall of Fame

A cell for every player every season, with a figure in their armour, a plaque and their whole inventory to look at, locked and view-only.

Rollover

Reminders before a planned end, a countdown at a live event, everyone archived and reset, and a launch to the new spawn with a fanfare.

Old world

Zones lock the old world until Homecoming; ender chests wait in a vault until then. Nothing anyone built is ever wiped.

Seasons Web

An optional live dashboard on your own server for phones, iPads and second screens: the season clock, who's online, the event feed, the map, leaderboards, profiles and the Hall.
