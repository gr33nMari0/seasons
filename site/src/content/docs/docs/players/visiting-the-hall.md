At the end of every season, everything you carry goes on display in your own cell in the Hall of Fame: your armour on a figure in your skin, the rest in a chest anyone can look in, and your season card on the wall. It's view-only forever.

## Getting there

- **From a bed:** stand within 3 blocks of any bed and type `/hof`, or sneak and right-click the bed with an empty hand.
- **By a link:** click a Hall link (a sign, a button, floating text) wherever your server has put one. Links skip the bed.
- `/hof <season>` goes to that season's gallery; `/hof <player>` to a player's cell.

If `/hof` is refused, the message says why: a monster nearby, recent damage, falling, standing in lava, or no bed near.

## Looking around

- Click a figure or an archive chest to see everything that player carried, with full names, enchantments, trims and durability. You can look, never take.
- Inside the Hall you're in adventure mode: no dropping or picking up items, no ender pearls.
- Click links in the hub to go to a season's spawn or anywhere else your server offers.

## Getting back

`/hof leave` takes you back to exactly where you were, checked for safety first. If lava, fire or monsters appeared there while you were away, you go to your bed, the nearest safe spot, or the season spawn, and you're told why. You get a few seconds of damage immunity on arrival.

## Your cell

When the decorating window opens (usually a week before the season ends), you're given a cell and told which.

- **Decorate it** in survival, with your own blocks. Chests, barrels, shulker boxes and decorated pots are fine; only you can open them while decorating, and afterwards they're on display, view-only, forever.
- **Swap** with a friend: both of you run `/hof swap <player>` within a minute.
- **Gates:** where cells have gates, `/hof gate open` or `/hof gate shut` opens or shuts yours.
- When the season ends you wake up in your cell, with a little time to arrange things around your new figure. Then it locks.

## Where your stuff goes

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Everything you carry | Your cell, view-only |
| Your ender chest | Your [ender vault](/docs/players/your-vault/), opened at Homecoming |
| Your registered pets | With you, to the new spawn (`/pet add`; see [Pets](/docs/owners/pets/)) |
| Your bases and builds | Where they are, locked until Homecoming, then yours again |

`/season info` shows the phase, the planned end, your cell and your archive.
