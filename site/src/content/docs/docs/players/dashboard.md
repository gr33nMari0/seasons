If your server runs Seasons Web, it has a live dashboard: the season clock, who's online, the event feed, the map, leaderboards, your profile and season cards, and the Hall of Fame.

## Finding it

Go to the [connect page](/connect/) and type the address you play on, such as `play.example.com`. It finds your server's dashboard and takes you there. Your server may also give you a direct link.

## Logging in

1. On the dashboard, choose **Log in**. It shows a code, such as `K7QX-2M`.
2. In Minecraft, type `/web link K7QX-2M`.
3. The dashboard logs you in. No passwords, ever.

You stay logged in on that device for 30 days. `/web sessions` lists your devices; `/web logout` ends one, and `/web logout all` ends them all.

## Your own dashboards

Choose **Edit layout** to make the dashboard yours:

- **Drag** a widget by its handle and **resize** it from its corner.
- **Add** any widget you're allowed to see from the tray, or remove one.
- With a keyboard: focus a widget, then the arrow keys move it and shift with the arrows resizes it.
- Make **several dashboards** ("Second screen", "Build night") and switch between them from the tabs.
- **Copy layout** gives a code you can paste on another device.

Layouts are kept in your browser on that device. **Reset** goes back to the server's default.

## Sharing

The **Share** button on any page copies its link, or opens your device's share sheet. Shared links show a preview: your server's season, a player's profile card, a gallery. Previews only show what anyone could see without logging in.

## The second screen

`/screen` is made for a tablet on a stand or a spare monitor: big type, always dark, always up to date, and it keeps the screen awake.

## Your privacy

- The dashboard never shows coordinates: only the dimension and the area you're in.
- `/web privacy` hides you from the leaderboards; run it again to show yourself.
- Your login lives on your server's dashboard, never on the public site.
