At the end of a season your ender chest is sealed into your **ender vault**, so it isn't a way to carry things into the new season.

- **Before Homecoming:** `/vault` shows what's in it, read-only.
- **After Homecoming:** `/vault` lets you take everything back. Click an item to take it. If your inventory is full, nothing is lost: the item stays in the vault.
- **Returned items:** if a curator returns something to you, or you slew the dragon and couldn't take the egg, it's on the **Returned** page, and you can take it at once, even before Homecoming.

Vaults from several seasons show one page each. You can never put anything into the vault.
