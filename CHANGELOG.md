# Changelog

Every behaviour change gets an entry. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and both plugins use [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The Hall of Fame (M1 part 3): galleries, cells and anchors in `<world>/seasons/galleries/<id>.yml`, validated on load and on `/season reload`, and tagged by command with `/hof gallery create|bounds|entry|exit|info` and `/hof cell new|bounds|anchor|unanchor|assign|unassign|info`.
- The decorating window, kept in `season.json`: it opens `decorating.opensBefore` (7 days) before the planned end or with `/season decorate open`, which hands everyone who played the season a random free cell and tells them which; later players get one at login or when archived. `/hof swap <player>` swaps cells when both ask within 60 seconds. Owners are in survival in their own cell while they may decorate, and everyone else in the Hall is in adventure and can't build. Cells lock at the end of the countdown, before anyone is archived; owners then get `decorating.afterArchive` (15 minutes) after their archive, until `/season decorate close` or launch.
- Displays: archived players are sent to their cell, where a mannequin wears their archived gear in that season's skin and a plaque shows "completed / visible" advancements. Archive anchors and figures open the archive view; every interaction with a display is cancelled, and the items displays show are tagged copies. `/hof reconcile <gallery>` and `/hof restore <player> <season>` redraw them, and `/season unarchive` removes them.
- `/hof import <gallery> [cell] [--dry-run]` turns hand-built Season 1 cells into archive records: one page per container, a mannequin in the player head's skin, trophies from item frames, and the containers kept as empty archive anchors. The dry run writes `import-<gallery>.txt` and flags cells whose owner isn't clear until `/hof cell assign`. Each cell is journalled and checked, and stops without changing anything if the cell changed since the scan; `/hof import undo <gallery> [cell]` puts every item back where it was. `/season unarchive` refuses imported records.
- `/season end --dry-run` reports a Hall cell shortfall; `/season info` shows your cell.
- Archiving (M1 part 2): at `/season end` every online player's inventory, armour, off hand, cursor and crafting grid, ender chest (sealed in the vault by default), XP, advancements (with completed / visible counts), statistics since the season began, furthest block, final position and skin go into `data/archives/s<n>/<uuid>.dat`, vanilla NBT with its DataVersion. They are then cleared and reset: XP, effects, fire, the respawn point and every advancement except recipe unlocks (all per `resets`), with full health and hunger. A player being archived can't do anything until it finishes, and a player who logs in can't do anything until their catch-up and any recovery have run. Cursor and crafting-grid items go back into the inventory first; whatever doesn't fit stays on the player until the inventory is cleared, and is archived as overflow. An existing archive file is never overwritten: it is moved aside as `<uuid>.superseded-<ms>.dat`.
- Every archive step is journaled after an fsync, and a crash at any step is finished when the player next logs in, so each item ends up exactly once. An inventory that neither matches the archive nor is empty is left alone and reported in `/season status`.
- Login catch-up: a player who missed the end, or a whole season, is archived into the last season they played at their next login; after launch they then go straight to the new spawn. Players from before Seasons was installed are archived into the season `/season init` recorded.
- `/season launch [--dry-run]` (from the Intermission, once `/season spawn set` has set the next season's spawn): sets the world spawn, resets the dragon flag, creates the `seasons_deaths_s<n>` objective, and brings everyone online to the spawn in survival with a title, a sound, a firework (which never hurts anyone) and 5 s of immunity (`hof.arrivalImmunity`). Players who were offline go there at their next login. A crash part-way through is finished at startup. Launch is refused while anyone online isn't archived into the season that ended or has an archive waiting for recovery.
- `/season archive <player>`, `/season unarchive <player> <season> [--force]` and `/season reprocess <player>`. Unarchive needs an empty inventory unless forced; anything in the way is kept in a displaced-items file in the archive format. An unarchived season is never archived again on its own once the player has entered a later season, and unarchiving an older season leaves later archives alone.
- The archive view: a read-only, paged view of an archive; every click type is refused.
- The tripwire: an archive-view copy found in a real inventory or on the ground is deleted within 30 s, logged and audited.
- The audit log (`audit/YYYY-MM.jsonl`) and player records (`data/players/<uuid>.json`).
- The furthest block placed from 0,0 this season in `/season status`, with archived and still-to-archive player counts.
- Season lifecycle (M1 part 1): Active (Frontier and Open stages), Ending (countdown, then archiving) and Intermission, kept in `<world>/seasons/data/season.json` and resumed after a restart. A restart during the end countdown cancels it and tells admins.
- `/season init <season>` records the season in progress on a server that has never run Seasons; refused once any season is recorded.
- `/season schedule end <time>` sets the planned end (ISO time with a time zone) in `seasons/<n>.yml`. Reminders go out at 7 days, 1 day, 1 hour and 10 minutes before it, in chat and on Discord; nothing ends at the planned time unless `schedule.autoEnd` is on.
- `/season end [--dry-run] [--now]` starts the boss-bar countdown (`schedule.endCountdown`, 10 minutes by default), or skips it, archives every online player, then reaches the Intermission.
- `/season cancel` stops the countdown before archiving starts.
- If `season.json` can't be saved when a season moves to archiving, nobody is archived and admins are told to fix it, restart and run `/season end` again.
- `/season status` shows the phase, planned end (server time and UTC), countdown, journal health and startup config problems; `/season info` shows players the phase and planned end.
- `config.yml` with every setting and its default, `secrets.yml` for webhook URLs (or the `SEASONS_DISCORD_WEBHOOK` and `SEASONS_ADMIN_WEBHOOK` environment variables), and validation. `/season reload` swaps every file in at once or keeps the previous config and reports file, line and message; `/season validate` only checks. A failed reload is also reported to the other online admins and the server log.
- The journal (`data/journal.log`): append-only, fsynced before the change it covers, with a checksum per line, damaged-line repair, compaction and a crash-recovery framework that later milestones plug into.
- Discord webhook sender: posts in order on its own thread with backoff, never pings anyone, never logs the URL.
- Scenario harness: one fresh server per scenario, scenarios with several sessions and a restart between them, and an https webhook receiver.
- M0 skeleton: Gradle build with Java 25, Error Prone, NullAway and Spotless; the `api`, `core`, `integrations`, `adapters/paper`, `web` and `testkit` modules.
- The core's ports, implemented by the Paper adapter, with declared adapter capabilities.
- `/season status` (ops): plugin version, season, and any adapter capability gaps. Text comes from `messages.yml`.
- `paper-plugin.yml` for Seasons and Seasons Web, with every permission node and its default.
- Scenario harness: `./gradlew scenarioTest` starts Paper with run-paper and drives it with Mineflayer bots; the NBT neutrality test opens item data written by the adapter with a plain NBT reader.
- Build and release workflows, Dependabot.
