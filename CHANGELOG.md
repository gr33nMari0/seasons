# Changelog

Every behaviour change gets an entry. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and both plugins use [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Seasons Web serves its API (M7 part 1): turn it on with `enabled: true` in `plugins/SeasonsWeb/web.yml`. It listens on `127.0.0.1:8019` by default (any port you choose), behind a Cloudflare Tunnel or HTTPS proxy, and refuses to listen beyond localhost without an https `public-url`. It offers `/.well-known/seasons.json`, the season, who's online, the Hall, profiles, leaderboards, waypoints, links, server health for `seasons.web.admin`, and a live event stream, each with a visibility setting in `web.yml`.
- Logging in to the dashboard with a code: `/web link <code>`, with `/web sessions`, `/web logout [id|all]` and `/web privacy` to hide yourself from leaderboards.
- Seasons API 1.2: snapshots of the season and who's online, profiles, galleries, archived gear as text, leaderboards, waypoints and events, for Seasons Web and other plugins.
- `api.refresh` and `api.afkAfter` in `config.yml`.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added

- A startup summary in the server log: the version, the season and its planned end, the Hall of Fame, the journal and player records, Discord and BlueMap, and whether setup needs attention. `logging.startupSummary: false` turns it off.
- One log line for each season milestone: a season recorded, the end and its countdown, everyone archived (one line, not one per player), a player archived at login, the launch, zone unlocks and Homecoming, reloads, curation, the dragon kill, Hall repairs and shutdown. A line when BlueMap is hooked.
- `logging.debug` in `config.yml` switches on detailed lines for chasing a problem, per area: `hall`, `archive`, `zones`, `discord`, `maps` or `commands`.
- `/seasons` works as an alias of `/season`, and `/season info` on a server with no season says Seasons is idle until `/season init`.

### Changed

- Every Seasons log line is labelled `[Seasons]`; some were labelled with a Java class name.
- Repeated warnings are throttled: a tripwire hit at most once a minute per player, and a failing Discord webhook at most once every five minutes, each with a count of those left out. Discord retries, logins and other per-player detail are debug lines only.
- Notes to admins that need nothing done, such as the spawn loading at launch, no longer go to the server log as warnings.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added

- bStats metrics (M6): Seasons reports as SeasonsMC (35499) and Seasons Web as SeasonsMC Web (35500), with Seasons' anonymous charts: whether Seasons Web is installed, the cell assignment mode, the cell access mode and how many seasons the server has run. No player names, coordinates or addresses. `enabled: false` in `plugins/bStats/config.yml` turns bStats off for every plugin.
- A configuration reference, `docs/config.md`, generated from the code with the bundled `config.yml`'s comments (M6).
- Releases are published to Hangar and Modrinth as well as GitHub Releases; a version with a hyphen is a beta (M6).

- The hub (post-M5): `/hof hall hub` marks the Hall's centre with two wand corners, and `/hof hall spawn` sets the Hall spawn in it, where plain `/hof` always lands. Both live in the world's `seasons/hall.yml` with the Hall's exits (`/hof hall exit`) and links. The Hall is the hub plus every gallery, so walking between the hub and its wings is staying in the Hall, and the hub is protected, exempt from zones and cut out of them on the map. Nothing writes `hall.yml` while it is left out after a failed load, the wand's link included.
- Links: `/hof link add <id> [hall|season <n>] [x y z]` makes any block (a button, a sign, a pressure plate) a link to the Hall spawn, a season's spawn or a spot set with `/hof link to`; clicking it, or stepping on it, takes the player there. A link to the Hall needs no bed; a link into locked land is refused until it opens; links out of the Hall wait during the Intermission. Link blocks outside the Hall are protected like Hall blocks. A link block outside the Hall stays when the block holding it up is broken; `/hof leave` back onto a pressure plate link doesn't take it again until the player steps off; standing on a link does nothing while the player is being archived.
- `hof.sealed` (default `intermission`): leaving the Hall by walking, falling or another plugin's teleport just leaves, except during the Intermission; `always` keeps the old behaviour. `hof.leaveWithoutSpot: season-spawn` sends `/hof leave` with no saved spot to the season's spawn, as after a respawn at a Hall spawn that is the world spawn. `launch.setWorldSpawn: false` keeps the world spawn at launch. `archive.arrival: hub` wakes everyone at the Hall spawn at season end instead of their cell.
- Gates in a cell's doorway: a gate's corners may sit just outside the cell's bounds. With `cells.gates: open` (the default) a gate is open until shut by an admin (`/hof cell gate`, `/hof gallery gate`) or its owner (`/hof gate`, `seasons.hof.gate`); `auto` keeps the old behaviour. A gate waits for its cell to be empty before it shuts, and with `cells.moveOut: false` (the default) nobody is moved out of a cell. A gate waits while any part of a player is in its cell or plane, checked again just before the blocks are set.
- Builders: players in creative mode build anywhere in the Hall as bypass holders do (`hof.creativeBuilds`), any block included; decoration stays as placed and does nothing for visitors.
- `/hof hall check` reports wings that don't touch the hub, odd-sized cells, galleries without an entry, cells without bounds or a figure, and links leading nowhere, with each gallery's cells and free cells. `/hof cell new <gallery>` with no id takes the next free `c<n>`. The wand offers a link, and a gate corner beside a cell, on blocks in the Hall outside every cell, and finds a cell from its floor.

- The wand (M5): `/hof wand` gives Hall admins a marked `wand.item` (default `blaze_rod`). Sneaking, a left click and a right click select two corners: after `/hof cell new` the first selection becomes that new cell's bounds (once; the wand never moves bounds a cell has), and `/hof cell bounds`, `/hof gallery bounds` and `/season zone add <id> box` or `outside box` with no corners take it, in its dimension. New cell bounds that would leave one of its anchors outside are refused. A plain right click on a block in a cell opens a menu of anchors to put there, with an egg plinth in a museum. A wand click never breaks, opens or uses a block.
- Templates and stamping (M5): build a cell with structure blocks in DATA mode as `seasons:figure`, `archive`, `plaque`, `trophy`, `entry` or `gate` markers (an archive marker stands on the block it anchors) and save it with a structure block as `seasons:<name>`, or drop a `.nbt` into the world's `seasons/templates/`. `/hof stamp <template> <gallery> <count> [--step x y z] [--rotate 0|90|180|270]` checks every copy, then places them where only air was, turns the markers into anchors of new cells, turned with the copy, and journals the stamp so a crash finishes it at startup. Templates holding items (a loot table, a dropped item or a pick-up arrow counts) or blocks the Hall doesn't allow (spawners included) are refused, and a copy placed again in recovery replaces its own entities rather than doubling them. Two templates ship in the jar: `alcove` and `plinth`. While a stamp runs, and until recovery finishes one that stopped, its gallery's bounds, entry, exits, launch button and cell bounds can't change and no cell can be made in it.
- Curation (M4 part 3): `/hof curate <player> <season>`, or sneak-clicking a cell's archive anchor or figure, opens the curator view of one archive, every click refused as in the archive view. A click on an item offers the actions the curator holds a node for (`seasons.curate.view`, `.museum`, `.return`, `.take`, `.destroy`, ops by default, or `seasons.curate.*` for a curator group): put it on a free museum plinth with a plaque crediting its owner, return it to the owner's Returned page, take it without the archived tag, or destroy it into the recycle bin for 30 days, where `/hof curate undo <id>` puts it back in its slot. The curator types the reason in chat, which nobody else sees (`curate.requireReason`, default true).
- Each curation is a journalled transaction (`CURATE_STARTED`, `CURATE_DONE`) with the item kept aside until it is delivered: a full inventory or a plinth taken since puts it back, and a crash at any step leaves it exactly once. Each is audited with the item's snapshot, owner, season, slot, destination, curator and reason, posted to the admin webhook, told to the owner in-game (at their next login if offline) and posted, without the reason, to the public channel. The figure is drawn again without gear taken from it.
- Discord: the season's end and the launch are posted to the public channel. The admin webhook gets tripwire hits, reconciliation repairs, recovery runs and curation actions, gathered two seconds at a time into one post. Each webhook has its own sender thread and queue, so one Discord rate-limits never holds up the other.
- A dragon egg every season (M4 part 1). With `dragon.newEggEachSeason`, launch clears the End's previously-killed flag, or does so when the End loads if it isn't loaded then. Seasons tracks every player who damages the End's dragon, by whoever caused the damage, arrows and crystals included. The season's first kill makes its slayer the player behind the final blow, or else whoever dealt the most damage; with nobody, the egg stays on the portal as in vanilla. The slayer gets a fanfare (a title and sound for everyone, fireworks over the exit portal, a broadcast, a Discord post) and the "slew the ender dragon" season first.
- The egg is taken off the exit portal as it appears and given to the slayer, or kept on the Returned page of their vault if their inventory is full or they have left. Each step is journalled (`EGG_DELIVERED`) and a crash at any of them is finished at startup. `dragon.eggTo: portal` leaves it on the portal.
- The museum: `/museum add <anchor> [label]` puts the op's held item on a museum plinth, shown as a copy with "label · Season · credit"; `/museum remove <anchor>` gives it back. Both are journalled (`MUSEUM_STARTED`, `MUSEUM_DONE`), recovered after a crash and audited. Plinths are museum cells with a trophy anchor, kept in `museum.yml` and `data/museum/`.
- The dragon egg is frozen on the exit portal from the kill until Seasons takes it, so nobody can click, push or blow it away in between; an egg that leaves the portal anyway is reported and the slayer is given none, rather than a second egg. After a crash, Seasons sets the End's previously-killed flag again and takes off an egg the vault already holds, since the End can roll back to before both.
- `/museum add` and `remove` are refused on a plinth whose last change a crash interrupted, until recovery settles it.
- A museum cell can have several trophy anchors, such as an egg wall: each egg plinth shows its own season's egg.
- The egg wall: a museum trophy anchor with `egg: <season>` shows that season's dragon egg with "Season 3 · slain by Taran" once the season has ended. `/season end --dry-run` warns when the season has no egg plinth.

### Changed

- Displays are drawn a few at a time, within 2 ms a tick, so a rollover no longer draws every cell in one tick (A43). A cell being drawn again keeps its figure throughout.
- The bundled `config.yml` describes every setting on its own line, with its choices; nested settings such as `hof.entry` and `wand.outlines` are written as sections rather than one-line maps. Defaults are unchanged, and existing files keep working.

### Security

- Archiving takes what sits in an open anvil's, stonecutter's, smithing table's, grindstone's, enchanting table's, loom's, cartography table's, beacon's or villager's input slots, with the cursor and crafting grid; before, a full inventory and an item on the cursor kept such a menu open, and closing it after the clear handed its inputs back for the new season. Anything that still turns up in the inventory after the clear goes into the record, never back to the player, and is audited as `late-items` (security review H1).
- While a player is held for archiving, unarchiving, a vault withdrawal or a curation, XP orbs stay on the ground, nothing they carry is mended or worn down, and their commands (another plugin's included) are refused, unless they hold `seasons.admin.season`; any of these could change the inventory between the copy and the clear and fail the archive (security review M1).
- Archiving journals `ARCHIVE_CLEARING` just before it clears a player, and crash recovery takes an empty inventory as already cleared only after that entry; before, emptying the inventory into a chest after a failed archive let recovery mark it done while the vault held a copy of the ender chest. A vault page whose archive is still open can't be withdrawn from (security review H3).
- An archive that stops before the clear while the server runs on is put back at once: its vault page removed, `ARCHIVE_ABORTED` journalled and its file moved aside, so the ender chest never exists twice. A player whose archive fails, or who is busy when it reaches them, is no longer let go with everything they carry: they are held as pending and archived again every 10 seconds until it goes through; admins hear once, and again if it is stuck after 6 tries. Before, a busy player was skipped at season end without a word (security review H2).
- Vault clicks: a click whose item wouldn't fit is refused before anything is journalled or written, at most one withdrawal runs and one waits per player, and the messages and audit entries a burst of clicks causes are limited. Before, every click with a full inventory journalled and rewrote the vault file twice and wrote an audit line, so click spam could fill the disk and stall Seasons' writes, the season's end included. Looking up a player's open operations no longer reads the whole journal on the server thread (security review M2).
- The tripwire looks inside bundles and shulker boxes: copies shown in the archive view, the vault and on figures are tagged inside too; a tagged copy inside an untagged bundle or shulker box is taken out of it at the next check, the rest kept; an archived copy can never be placed as a block; and any container opened is checked as the player opens it (security review M3).
- A vault that only ever had items handed back to its Returned page now records the game's `DataVersion` like any other, so it opens with a plain NBT reader (found by `sec-vault-flood`).
- `/season reprocess <player> --restart` puts back a stuck archive whose player's inventory neither matches nor is empty, and archives them afresh from what they carry now.

## [1.0.0-beta.1] - 2026-11-20

### Added

- Finishing M0 to M3 (#19). A gallery file may set its own `assignment`, `access`, `renderer` and `decorating` (`lockAtEnd`, `afterArchive`); whatever it leaves out comes from `config.yml`.
- Access mode `bounds`: only a cell's owner, while they may decorate it, goes into the cell, by any route; anyone else found inside goes to the gallery's entry, and `/hof <player>` lands there. Refusals are audited.
- Access mode `gate`: a cell's two gate anchors mark a plane that is `cells.gateBlock` (default `iron_bars`) while the cell is locked and air while its owner may decorate it, each change journalled (`GATE_SET`) first. A gate is never set over a block with contents or over an anchor, and one that doesn't stay set is left alone and admins are told.
- `/hof claim [cell]` and `claim` assignment: during the window, a player who played the season claims a free cell by name or by standing in it; anyone who never claims gets a free cell when archived. Claims are audited.
- The armour-stand figure (`renderer: armour-stand`, or a platform without mannequins): arms, every slot locked, the player's head in the archived skin, the helmet on a trophy anchor. A cell may set its figure's `pose`.
- The launch button: `/hof gallery launch <gallery> [at|none]`; an admin presses it twice within 10 seconds during the Intermission to launch.
- `/season audit [player]` shows the last 10 audit entries; `/season info` shows the player's archive status.
- `config.yml` must name items the server knows, and a gate block that is solid, doesn't fall and holds nothing.

- More zone shapes (M3 part 4): `outside` (everything beyond a circle or a box, at every height), `y-above` and `y-below` (everything from a height up, or up to it, across the whole dimension), in season files and by `/season zone add <id> outside circle <radius>` (centred where the admin stands), `outside box <x1> <z1> <x2> <z2>`, `y-above <y>` and `y-below <y>`. They keep every rule box and cylinder zones have: entry by any route, the edge, the sweep and portals. The spawn and every spawn candidate must sit outside every zone, which for an `outside` zone means inside its circle or box. Season files naming these shapes are no longer refused.
- On BlueMap an `outside` zone is shown as a ring around its circle or box, reaching `max(2 × radius, 5000)` blocks beyond it, with the circle or box cut out; `y-above` and `y-below` zones aren't shown, since they would cover the whole map. Map Link copies BlueMap's areas onto Xaero's maps, but leaves out any larger than its `maxChunkArea` (questions 164).
- `zones.frontierBorder` (off by default): each player inside a locked `outside` circle sees a world border of their own, the square around the circle, visual only. Players in the Hall, in another dimension or holding `seasons.zone.bypass` see the world's border, which is never changed. It is sent again at once after a change of dimension or a respawn, when the game sends the world's own.
- `/season config get <path>` shows a setting of `config.yml` as it is in force, or every setting in a section; `/season config set <path> <value>` writes the value back into `config.yml` in place, keeping every other line and every comment except those between the items of a block list it replaces, adds a missing key under its section, and is validated, reloaded, put back if it doesn't take, and audited like every other command edit. Webhook URLs are never shown or set by command, and the refusal says that one typed into the command is now in the server log and should be rotated; nothing in `config.yml` but its settings is shown.

- BlueMap (M3 part 3): when BlueMap is installed, Seasons publishes a `Seasons` marker set to every map of each world: the spawn of every season that has launched, the next season's spawn candidates until it launches, the Hall where `/hof` lands, each museum, and every zone in force as an area styled by what it stops now (locked, no-build or announced), with the Hall cut out of it. Homecoming, launch and every edit change the map within a second, and everything is published again whenever BlueMap reloads. Without BlueMap nothing changes, and `/season status` says whether BlueMap is installed and how many markers it shows.
- Spawn candidates: `/season spawn candidate add <name>` marks where the admin stands as a candidate for the next season's spawn, `remove <name>` takes one away and `list` shows each with its distance from 0, 0 and which is the spawn. `/season spawn set <candidate>` sets the spawn to one, from the console too. Neither the spawn nor a candidate can be inside a zone, and `/season zone add` refuses a zone around either.
- An overworld spawn more than 24,000 blocks from 0, 0 is set with a warning that strongholds may be out of reach, which `/season reload`, `/season validate` and `/season end --dry-run` repeat.
- Shared waypoints: `/season marker add <set> <name>` saves the admin's position as a waypoint in `<world>/seasons/markers.yml`, shown on BlueMap as a marker set of its own, and `/season marker remove <set> <name>` takes it off; names are plain text, told apart ignoring case. A hand edit takes effect on `/season reload`. A `markers.yml` that fails to load at startup is left out, as a broken gallery file is, and nothing else is: the zones still load, `/season status` and admins joining hear about it, and the marker commands refuse until it is fixed and reloaded.
- Setting the spawn, spawn candidates and waypoints are audited as config changes, with the old value, the new one and who.

- The ender vault (M3 part 2): `/vault` shows the ender chests sealed at each rollover, one page per season with the newest first, then a Returned page. Season pages are read-only until Homecoming and withdraw-only after it, for everything, collectibles included; between seasons they are read-only again. The Returned page can be taken from at once. Each left or shift click on an item is one journalled transaction (`VAULT_WITHDRAW`, `VAULT_WITHDRAW_DONE`): the item leaves the vault file, then is given whole; with no room it stays in the vault, a failure part-way puts it back at once, and a crash part-way is settled at the player's next login. Nothing can be put in. Withdrawals are audited.
- Pets: `/pet add` registers the tamed animal a player looks at within 5 blocks (their own, without a chest, up to `pets.maxPerPlayer`), `/pet list` shows each with where it was last seen, and `/pet remove <name>` takes one off. When the owner reaches the next season's spawn, at launch or at their first login after it, each pet is found (its chunk loaded without stalling the tick), unleashed, dismounted and brought beside them; one carrying a chest stays behind, and the owner hears about each. A registered pet that dies is reported at once, or at the owner's next login, and dropped; one removed any other way is settled at travel, and a parrot on its owner's shoulder stays registered and comes with them. A pet inside a locked zone can't be registered from outside its edge before Homecoming. `/season status` shows pets still to travel.

- Zones over the old world (M3 part 1), kept in each season's file and in force from that season's launch. `/season zone add <id> cylinder <radius> [--max-y <y>] [--min-y <y>]` draws a circle around where the admin stands, and `/season zone add <id> box <x1> <y1> <z1> <x2> <y2> <z2>` a box; `/season zone mode`, `after`, `remove` and `list` change and show them. A new zone is `block` until Homecoming and `off` after it. The Hall is never in a zone.
- Block mode: nobody gets into a locked zone by walking, flying, riding, ender pearls, chorus fruit or portals, and a new nether portal is never made inside one. A player found inside (logged in there, ridden in, or in a zone drawn or locked around them) is put back where they last stood outside, or at the season spawn, dismounted, stopped and told why. From outside the edge nothing inside can be broken, placed, opened, used or emptied into, and pistons, hoppers, liquids, fire, growth, dispensers and explosions can't cross it; inside, nothing burns or is exploded. Holders of `seasons.zone.bypass` are exempt. Refusals are audited as `zone-blocked`.
- No-build mode: players walk in, open containers, use doors, buttons and levers, and trade, but can't place or break blocks, use buckets, place or break item frames, paintings, armour stands and cushions, or light TNT; explosions and fire there break and burn nothing. Announce mode broadcasts a player's first entry each day, and posts it to Discord with `zones.announceToDiscord`.
- Unlock conditions for zones and Homecoming: `manual` (the default), `date`, `after` launch, `dragonKilled`, `playersReachedEnd`, and `any` or `all` of them. `/season unlock <zone>` and `/season lock <zone>` override a zone's condition.
- Homecoming: `/season unlock homecoming`, or its condition coming true, switches every zone to its after-unlock mode in the same tick, with a title and a sound for everyone online, a broadcast, a Discord post and fireworks at the spawn, once.
- Review of #13: a player's hit on anything in a zone is decided wherever it is, so from a blocked zone's edge, or in a no-build zone, nobody knocks the item out of a frame or breaks an armour stand, a minecart or a boat; items lying in a blocked zone can't be picked up from outside it, nor reeled out with a fishing rod. Homecoming opens every zone, those locked again on their own included. A zone in a dimension the server doesn't have is refused on load. Refused entries, Homecoming, one zone unlocked or locked, and every zone edit (path, old and new value, who) go to the audit log. A player who arrives in a blocked zone through a portal is put back at once.
- The spawn must sit outside every zone: `/season zone add` and `/season spawn set` refuse otherwise, a season file that puts it inside one loads with a warning, `/season end --dry-run` names the zone, and `/season launch` is refused until one of them moves.

- Hall protection (M2 part 2). Inside a gallery nothing changes unless an owner is decorating their own cell, or a player holds `seasons.hof.bypass`. Visitors walk into any cell in adventure mode, open containers as view-only copies, sit on cushions and open wooden doors, trapdoors and fence gates, and change nothing else: no blocks, item frames, armour stands, cushions, buckets, signs, lecterns or levers. During the window an owner opens their own containers for real and picks up what they break in their cell; after the lock their containers open as copies for them too. Hoppers, droppers, dispensers, crafters, ender chests, hopper minecarts and lava never go in the Hall, nor any container not on `cells.containers`, and a container must be empty to break. Explosions, fire, pistons, flowing water, dispensers, hoppers, growth, mob spawning and projectiles can't change the Hall, and nothing in it takes damage but from `/kill` and the void.
- Refused Hall interactions and bypass edits are written to the audit log (`hall-blocked`, `hall-bypass`), a repeat within 10 seconds counted rather than written again.
- After their archive, in `decorating.afterArchive`, an owner still arranges their cell but takes nothing out of it: their containers open as view-only copies, broken blocks drop nothing, nothing is picked up, and armour stands and item frames keep what they hold.
- A chest can't join another into a double chest across a cell's edge or the Hall's; a double chest already across one opens as a view-only copy and no hopper moves items through it.
- Reconciliation: when a Hall chunk's entities load, every 5 minutes while loaded, and on `/hof reconcile`, each cell's figure, plaque and trophies are compared with its record; missing ones are drawn, stray ones removed (one of the cell's own moved out of it too) and changed gear put back, within about 1 ms a tick, each repair audited.
- The tripwire also finds archived copies in item frames, on armour stands and other entities, on the ground and in Hall containers as their chunks load, and in hoppers anywhere.

- Season firsts (M1 part 4): the first player each season to complete each advancement, reach the Nether or the End, or carry a diamond, an elytra or an item on `firsts.rareItems` is announced in chat and on Discord, once, and kept in `season.json`. `/season firsts` lists them.
- The season card: right-clicking a cell's plaque anchor opens a book with the player's highlights (play time, deaths, kills, distance travelled, diamonds and ancient debris mined, blocks placed and mined, advancements, furthest block, where they ended), their season firsts and every General-tab statistic as the season's difference. No item is involved.
- A gallery file that can't be loaded at startup is left out on its own instead of sending every file back to the defaults. `/season status` shows it, admins are told as they join, and `/season end` and `/season launch` are refused until it is fixed and `/season reload` succeeds.
- The Hall of Fame (M1 part 3): galleries, cells and anchors in `<world>/seasons/galleries/<id>.yml`, validated on load and on `/season reload`, and tagged by command with `/hof gallery create|bounds|entry|exit|info` and `/hof cell new|bounds|anchor|unanchor|assign|unassign|info`.
- The decorating window, kept in `season.json`: it opens `decorating.opensBefore` (7 days) before the planned end or with `/season decorate open`, which hands everyone who played the season a random free cell and tells them which; later players get one at login or when archived. `/hof swap <player>` swaps cells when both ask within 60 seconds. Owners are in survival in their own cell while they may decorate, and everyone else in the Hall is in adventure and can't build. Cells lock at the end of the countdown, before anyone is archived; owners then get `decorating.afterArchive` (15 minutes) after their archive, until `/season decorate close` or launch.
- Displays: archived players are sent to their cell, where a mannequin wears their archived gear in that season's skin and a plaque shows "completed / visible" advancements. Archive anchors and figures open the archive view; every interaction with a display is cancelled, and the items displays show are tagged copies. `/hof reconcile <gallery>` and `/hof restore <player> <season>` redraw them, and `/season unarchive` removes them.
- `/hof import <gallery> [cell] [--dry-run]` turns hand-built Season 1 cells into archive records: one page per container, a mannequin in the player head's skin, trophies from item frames, and the containers kept as empty archive anchors. The dry run writes `import-<gallery>.txt` and flags cells whose owner isn't clear until `/hof cell assign`. Each cell is journalled and checked, and stops without changing anything if the cell changed since the scan; `/hof import undo <gallery> [cell]` puts every item back where it was. `/season unarchive` refuses imported records.
- `/season end --dry-run` reports a Hall cell shortfall; `/season info` shows your cell.
- Archiving (M1 part 2): at `/season end` every online player's inventory, armour, off hand, cursor and crafting grid, ender chest (sealed in the vault by default), XP, advancements (with completed / visible counts), statistics since the season began, furthest block, final position and skin go into `data/archives/s<n>/<uuid>.dat`, vanilla NBT with its DataVersion. They are then cleared and reset: XP, effects, fire, the respawn point and every advancement except recipe unlocks (all per `resets`), with full health and hunger. A player being archived can't do anything until it finishes, and a player who logs in can't do anything until their catch-up and any recovery have run. Cursor and crafting-grid items go back into the inventory first; whatever doesn't fit stays on the player until the inventory is cleared, and is archived as overflow. An existing archive file is never overwritten: it is moved aside as `<uuid>.superseded-<ms>.dat`.
- Every archive step is journaled after an fsync, and a crash at any step is finished when the player next logs in, so each item ends up exactly once. An inventory that neither matches the archive nor is empty is left alone and reported in `/season status`.
- Login catch-up: a player who missed the end, or a whole season, is archived into the last season they played at their next login; after launch they then go straight to the new spawn. Players from before Seasons was installed are archived into the season `/season init` recorded.
- `/season launch [--dry-run]` (from the Intermission, once `/season spawn set` has set the next season's spawn): sets the world spawn, resets the dragon flag, creates the `seasons_deaths_s<n>` objective, and brings everyone online to the spawn in survival with a title, a sound, a firework (which never hurts anyone) and 5 s of immunity (`hof.arrivalImmunity`). Players who were offline go there at their next login. A crash part-way through is finished at startup. Launch is refused while anyone online isn't archived into the season that ended or has an archive waiting for recovery.
- `/season archive <player>`, `/season unarchive <player> <season> [--force]` and `/season reprocess <player>`. Unarchive needs an empty inventory unless forced; anything in the way is kept in a displaced-items file in the archive format. An unarchived season is never archived again on its own once the player has entered a later season, and unarchiving an older season leaves later archives alone.
- The archive view: a read-only, paged view of an archive; every click type is refused.
- The tripwire: an archive-view copy found in a real inventory or on the ground is deleted within 30 s, logged and audited.
- The audit log (`audit/YYYY-MM.jsonl`) and player records (`data/players/<uuid>.json`).
- The furthest block placed from 0,0 this season in `/season status`, with archived and still-to-archive player counts.
- Season lifecycle (M1 part 1): Active (Frontier and Open stages), Ending (countdown, then archiving) and Intermission, kept in `<world>/seasons/data/season.json` and resumed after a restart. A restart during the end countdown cancels it and tells admins.
- `/season init <season>` records the season in progress on a server that has never run Seasons; refused once any season is recorded.
- `/season schedule end <time>` sets the planned end (ISO time with a time zone) in `seasons/<n>.yml`. Reminders go out at 7 days, 1 day, 1 hour and 10 minutes before it, in chat and on Discord; nothing ends at the planned time unless `schedule.autoEnd` is on.
- `/season end [--dry-run] [--now]` starts the boss-bar countdown (`schedule.endCountdown`, 10 minutes by default), or skips it, archives every online player, then reaches the Intermission.
- `/season cancel` stops the countdown before archiving starts.
- If `season.json` can't be saved when a season moves to archiving, nobody is archived and admins are told to fix it, restart and run `/season end` again.
- `/season status` shows the phase, planned end (server time and UTC), countdown, journal health and startup config problems; `/season info` shows players the phase and planned end.
- `config.yml` with every setting and its default, `secrets.yml` for webhook URLs (or the `SEASONS_DISCORD_WEBHOOK` and `SEASONS_ADMIN_WEBHOOK` environment variables), and validation. `/season reload` swaps every file in at once or keeps the previous config and reports file, line and message; `/season validate` only checks. A failed reload is also reported to the other online admins and the server log.
- The journal (`data/journal.log`): append-only, fsynced before the change it covers, with a checksum per line, damaged-line repair, compaction and a crash-recovery framework that later milestones plug into.
- Discord webhook sender: posts in order on its own thread with backoff, never pings anyone, never logs the URL.
- Scenario harness: one fresh server per scenario, scenarios with several sessions and a restart between them, and an https webhook receiver.
- M0 skeleton: Gradle build with Java 25, Error Prone, NullAway and Spotless; the `api`, `core`, `integrations`, `adapters/paper`, `web` and `testkit` modules.
- The core's ports, implemented by the Paper adapter, with declared adapter capabilities.
- `/season status` (ops): plugin version, season, and any adapter capability gaps. Text comes from `messages.yml`.
- `paper-plugin.yml` for Seasons and Seasons Web, with every permission node and its default.
- Scenario harness: `./gradlew scenarioTest` starts Paper with run-paper and drives it with Mineflayer bots; the NBT neutrality test opens item data written by the adapter with a plain NBT reader.
- Build and release workflows, Dependabot.

### Changed

- Plain `/hof` lands at the Hall spawn and is refused without one, rather than at the newest gallery's entry. A season spawn or spawn candidate inside the Hall is refused by the commands, warned about on reload and in the end dry run, and stops launch.
- A zone, spawn, spawn candidate or waypoint edit by command that doesn't take (the files then fail to load, say because of another file's unreloaded hand edit) puts its file back as it was, so it never takes effect later unaudited, and the admin hears the reason worded as `/season reload` words it.
- `/season unarchive` restores a sealed ender chest from its vault page as it is now, so nothing withdrawn since comes back twice, and holds the player from before it reads the page, so no withdrawal can slip in between.
- `launch.requireManual: false` is refused on load, since nothing launches by itself: launch is by `/season launch` or the launch button.
- A player who logs in inside a blocked zone goes to the season spawn; where they last stood outside every blocked zone is kept in their record.
